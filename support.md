# Multo Support

Need help with Multo? Most questions are answered below. If you're still
stuck, email **multomultply@gmail.com** and I'll get back to you.

## Turning on voice answers

Voice answers are off by default. Tap the microphone icon, give permission
when asked, and download the voice model (about 1.5 GB, one time). After
that you can say answers out loud instead of typing.

## Why is the voice model so large?

The voice model runs entirely on your device, so your voice never leaves
your iPhone or iPad. That on-device model is about 1.5 GB. It only downloads
if you turn voice on. Skip it and Multo works fine with typed answers.

## Freeing up space

To remove the voice model and reclaim the space, go to
**Settings → Voice Model → Clear voice model**. You can re-download it later.

## Adding another learner

On the profile screen, tap **Add** to create a new profile. Each profile
keeps its own streaks, struggle tables, and settings, so more than one
person can use Multo on the same device without mixing up progress.

## Changing the language

Open **Settings** and choose English, Afrikaans, German, Spanish, or
Italian. This changes both the interface and the voice recognition language.

## Does Multo work offline?

Yes. Once installed (and once the voice model is downloaded, if you use
voice), Multo works fully offline.

## Privacy

Multo collects nothing: no accounts, no analytics, no ads, no tracking.
Full details are in the [privacy policy](https://sssushi-a.github.io/multo-privacy/).
