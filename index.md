# Privacy Policy for Multo

**Last updated:** 23 May 2026

Multo is an iOS app that helps children practise multiplication and division. This policy explains exactly what happens to data when you use Multo.

## The short version

Multo doesn't collect, transmit, or share any personal information. Everything happens on your device. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no tracking.

## What's stored on your device

The following stays on your iPhone or iPad and is never sent anywhere:

- **Profile names** you create (e.g. "Emma", "Liam").
- **Practice results** — which times tables a profile has practised, scores, and streaks.
- **Settings** — language, appearance, voice model state.

You can delete this data at any time by removing a profile inside the app, or by deleting the Multo app from your device.

## Microphone & speech recognition

If you turn on voice answers, Multo asks for permission to use the microphone. Audio is processed entirely on your device using:

- **WhisperKit** — an open-source speech recognition library that runs locally. Audio never leaves your device.
- **Apple's Speech framework** — when used, Multo configures it for on-device recognition only.

Recordings are not stored after they are transcribed. Nothing is uploaded to Apple, OpenAI, or anyone else.

## The voice model download

If you enable voice answers, Multo will — with your explicit consent — download a speech recognition model from Hugging Face's content delivery network. This is a one-time download of roughly 1.5 GB. Hugging Face will see the standard network metadata of any HTTPS request (such as your IP address); Multo does not send any personal information along with the download. You can remove the model at any time from **Settings → Voice Model → Clear voice model**.

## Children's privacy

Multo is designed for children. Because Multo collects no personal information, transmits nothing off-device, and contains no advertising or third-party tracking, it is consistent with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the children's provisions of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

A profile name is the only thing a child types that resembles personal data, and it never leaves the device.

## No analytics, no ads, no tracking

Multo contains no third-party analytics SDKs (e.g. Google Analytics, Firebase, Mixpanel), no advertising, no marketing trackers, and no crash reporting that transmits data off-device. If you choose to share crash diagnostics with Apple through iOS Settings, those reports go through Apple's standard process — not through Multo.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will replace this page. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect any change.
